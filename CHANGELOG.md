## [October 23, 2026](https://github.com/OpsLevel/opslevel-agent/compare/v2026.7.8...v2026.10.23)
### Security
* Bumped `golang.org/x/net` from v0.55.0 to v0.60.0 and the Go toolchain to 1.26.10 to patch the critical HTTP/2 double flow control refund vulnerability (GO-2026-6612, CVE-2026-78663) and the crypto/x509 compromised intermediate certificates issue (GO-2026-6809).
### Dependency
* Bumped the minimum required Go version from 1.25.0 to 1.26.10. Building the agent from source now requires Go 1.26.10+.
* Bumped `k8s.io/api`, `k8s.io/apimachinery` and `k8s.io/client-go` from v0.33.3 to v0.37.1 together, and grouped `k8s.io/*` updates in Dependabot so they are always bumped in lockstep.
* Bump crazy-max/ghaction-import-gpg from 6.3.0 to 7.0.0
* Bump docker/login-action from 3.7.0 to 4.6.0
* Bump github.com/go-resty/resty/v2 from 2.16.5 to 2.17.2
* Bump github.com/rs/zerolog from 1.34.0 to 1.35.1
* Bump codecov/codecov-action from 5.5.4 to 7.1.1
* Bump submodules/opslevel-go from `f519f64` to `7304fbb`
